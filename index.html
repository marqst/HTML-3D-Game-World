3D Labyrinth prototype

Made entirely with HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JS. No use of canvas or any external animation or 3D graphic libraries. Only DIVs and CSS 3D transforms. This is Proof of Concept and Work in Progress.

Created by a gamer, but not a game developer :) Result has the vibes of old games from the 90's. Right angles only :) And not optimised performance-wise

This prototype uses many modern HTML, CSS and JS features, which, at the moment of creating it, were not Baseline and may not work on all browsers!

How to play

Just walk around and enjoy :) To finish, walk into the yellow glowing spot in the labyrinth's centre.

Controls

  • Up Arrow / W / Numpad 8 - move forward
  • Down Arrow / S / Numpad 2 - move back
  • Left Arrow / A / Numpad 4 or Mouse - turn left
  • Right Arrow / D / Numpad 6 or Mouse - turn right
  • Q / Numpad 1 - strafe left
  • E / Numpad 3 - strafe right
  • M - toggle between First Person / Top-down view
